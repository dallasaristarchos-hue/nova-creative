05 / Good to know

Quick answers.

Do you create the captions and post for me?

This service focuses on graphic design and delivery of the agreed files. Posting and account management are not included.

What should I send you?

Your business or page link, logo if you have one, brand colours, the text for the graphics and a few examples you like.

How much does it cost?

Each project is quoted based on the agreed scope. You will receive the price before deciding whether to proceed.

Are the portfolio examples real clients?

No. The projects shown here are clearly labelled fictional design concepts made to demonstrate style and creative direction.
